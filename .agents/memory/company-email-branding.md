---
name: Company email branding
description: Transactional emails use the company attached to the record, not a fixed parent-company name.
---

Company transactional emails must use each company's name, not a fixed “Grupo Joper” label. This includes the sender display name and company references in the message and subject.

**Why:** The user reported that emails were appearing as Grupo Joper instead of being renamed for each company.

**How to apply:** Resolve the company from the document's tenant, including public approval-triggered sends. Do not infer it from the sender's home company, which may differ after switching companies. Keep platform onboarding mail branded Nexxo; changing the verified sending address is separate from changing the display name.
