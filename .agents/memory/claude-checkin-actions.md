---
name: Claude check-in actions
description: User-requested behavior for voice-driven calls, agreements, email and follow-up through Claude.
---

The user wants to consult and register calls or events through check-ins using Claude voice commands, capturing the agreement and the customer's commitment automatically.

Email and follow-up must be selectable in the instruction, not automatic consequences of registering a call. The user selected both follow-up behaviors according to the instruction: keeping the check-in open and scheduling future follow-up when a date is provided.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested control over whether emails or follow-up happen, rather than treating every dictated contact as permission to send or schedule.

**How to apply:** Keep contact recording, email delivery and dated follow-up independently authorized. Do not close the check-in just to record a call or create its minute PDF. Do not invent a future appointment date.
